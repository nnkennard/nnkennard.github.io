I’m a final-year PhD candidate in Computer Science at the University of Massachusetts Amherst, advised by [Prof. Andrew McCallum](https://people.cs.umass.edu/~mccallum/){:target="_blank"}.
My research aims to develop principled ways to leverage large language models in the academic peer review process.
By drawing on theories from sociology of science, I aim to reduce the burden of peer review on scientists without disrupting the social mechanisms that underpin it. 

During my PhD, I've also had the opportunity to [teach computer science]({{"/teaching/" | relative_url}}) at the undergraduate level. As of Fall 2026, I am actively seeking a teaching-oriented tenure-track position in computer science.
